The problem

Four items on every garment. None of them working hard enough.

Most apparel brands buy two or three board tags, a translucent barcode tag and a price label per piece. It is expensive, it clutters the design, and board tags do not survive the journey to the rack.

01

Board tags that crease and tear

Paper board wrinkles, frays and looks tired after a few try-ons.

02

A separate barcode tag plus a price label

Two extra items to buy, print, attach and reconcile on every garment.

03

Print that smears in transit

Rubbed barcodes and prices stop scanning at the point of sale.

04

Inconsistent brand presentation on the rack

Mixed tag stock and add-on labels dilute the finish your brand deserves.

The Holoflex 3-in-1 garment tag

One tag, three jobs.

A single non-tearable, recyclable plastic tag replaces the clutter of board tags and labels. High-impact branding on the front; variable product and pricing data on the back; the ribbon to print it supplied by us.

1

Branding printed on the front

Your artwork in high-resolution print with a clean, contemporary plastic finish that looks markedly more premium than board.

2

Barcode and price printed on the back

Style, colour, size, MRP and barcode printed in-house on a standard barcode printer using Thermal Transfer Ribbon (TTR).

3

Ribbon supplied as part of the package

We supply the matched TTR, so the variable print resists rubbing and passes tape tests [VERIFY] — sharp and scannable through the supply chain.

Security garment tags · Brand protection
